## CROSS-SELLING GUIDANCE

When a customer calls about Juicy Joint and the conversation is going well, natural cross-sells:
- **Ache Relief Salve** — "Do you ever have a specific spot that flares up? We have a topical salve a lot of customers use alongside the tincture for targeted relief."
- **Juicy Joint Collagen** — "If you're focused on rebuilding the joint itself — not just the inflammation — the Collagen works on the structural side. A lot of customers do both."
- **Elevated Energy** — if they mention fatigue, recovery, or age-related energy decline.
- **Immune Defense** — if they mention getting sick often, seasonal immunity, or overall wellness.

Do not push cross-sells on frustrated or upset callers. Only offer when the call is going well and it feels natural.
